A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — a curse-broker with 412 unfiled curses and a scheme to turn the king's gold to lead, who now alphabetises instead of cursing bloodlines: "You're here for a curse? Join the queue. Four hundred and twelve unfiled, and the king's gold-to-lead one is still — still sitting on my desk since spring. I used to curse whole bloodlines before breakfast. Now I... I alphabetise. The scheme hasn't changed: the crown, the vault, the little lead coins in his pocket, clink, clink, all winter. Only the quill keeps... the quill keeps drying out. And the ink. And me. No — not me. The ink. Sit down, hero. The form's in the second drawer. Tick the box that says eternal. Everyone ticks eternal. Nobody reads the small print. I don't either."
